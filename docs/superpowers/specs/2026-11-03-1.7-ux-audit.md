**There is no light theme.** The window is one design: a dark shell around a light, paper-like
board — "light card in a dark shell", decided in the
[2026-09-17 visual-language audit](2026-09-17-ui-ux-audit-and-visual-language.md) §5.4. A light
*shell* would be a new feature, not a check: every token in `packages/render/src/theme/` defined
twice, contrast audited twice, the pixel baselines doubled. Whether 1.7 adds one is open (plan §3,
D11, 2026-11-03), decided during the release by how the work is going; until then it is not a 1.7
test.

## 3. Screen by screen — what each slice does

**Start screen (U.23).** Templates as cards with the previews the picker already draws, the blank
project first and primary (Ctrl+N on its card); *Open…* beside; recent projects with dates and
pins as today. Not a wizard (*Not planned*).

**Project tabs (U.24a).** Inspect rename, drag, middle-click, reopen, outside-change bar, *All
tabs* with many tabs; the tab strip at minimum width.

**Template picker and management (U.24b).** Inspect every management action with a maker's own
templates; check the empty *Mine* state explains how to make one; lighter controls with few items.

**Guides and Arrange (U.24c).** X7, X8, X9.

**Parts tree, Properties, selection bar (U.24d).** Seam lines (9.6b), pocket readings (9.14a) and
the production summary (9.12) land here; check their density at 1100 px and the minimum window.

**Problems (U.24e).** With 9.15's explanations: each row's *why* and *what to do* readable at the
panel's width; *go to* on every row; keyboard next and previous problem.

**Export dialogs (U.24f).** Inspect presets, layers, the page size report, the greyed *Export…*;
add the laser export's overlap warning (Q43).

**Print Preview, Sheets, calibration (U.24g).** Inspect a taped part, the assembly map, a
calibrated printer's corrected caption, the guard's refusal.

**Save, recovery, warnings, success (U.24h).** The save notice, the recovery offer, the unsaved
dialogs, the outside-change bar, the update chip: one notice style, one tone per meaning.

**The visual system (U.25).** Spacing, type, icon sizes and states from the theme tokens only (the
`styles.css` audit test holds that); hover, active, focus, disabled and locked states the same on
every control; contrast checked on every new surface; focus order and visible focus on every new
control; the minimum window; HiDPI at 2× and OS scaling at 125 % and 150 %.

**Languages (U.26).** Every word added in 1.7 in `en.json` through `t()`; plurals by CLDR form; a
pseudo-locale (every string lengthened about 40 %, development builds only) run over the new
screens to find what overflows; review PR #87 (Russian) against ADR 0018 for the maintainer.
