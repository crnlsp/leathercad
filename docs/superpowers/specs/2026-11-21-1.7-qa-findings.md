# 1.7 QA findings — functional and usability (2026-11-21)

Two independent QA passes over `develop` at b8b9b57, before 1.7's release candidate: a
**functional** tester (common paths, negative and absurd input, malformed files, trying to break
it) and a **usability** tester (UI, UX, wording, keyboard and screen reader, hard workflows). Both
drove the built app on Linux under a hidden screen with temporary config, printed nothing, and
reproduced each finding at least twice. Their tasks, QA1–QA11, are in the
[roadmap's 1.7 section](../../roadmap.md#stage-7--qa-fixes); each fix PR closes its rows here.

Severity as the [1.7 UX register](2026-11-03-1.7-ux-audit.md): **S1** loses work or blocks a
maker · **S2** wrong or confusing, often · **S3** now and then · **S4** cosmetic.

Not reported again: the UX register's X1–X40, *part* vs *piece*, sheet labels meeting at a very
small *Fit*, the known limitations in `getting-started.md`, `fitArcs`' 5e-15 overshoot under some
seeds, and the unchecked bytes of `writeFile`/`writeRecovery`/`keepUnsaved`.

**What held.** The repo's own journeys, export measurements, copy and paste, templates, tabs and
unsaved-changes specs passed (64/64) on this build; typed pieces exported at true size in PDF, SVG
and DXF, never scaled to fit; corrupt, truncated, future and odd files were refused with the
project kept; SVG `<use>` loops, NaN coordinates and entity attacks were refused; 60 undos and
redos returned exactly; one axe violation in about 35 scans.
